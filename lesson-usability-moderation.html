Usability Test Moderation

Learn how to facilitate usability sessions without accidentally teaching participants the answer. Good moderation creates a comfortable environment while protecting the quality of the evidence.

Learn

Facilitate, don't lead

The moderator sets context, observes and asks neutral follow-ups. The participant should do the task. Avoid rescuing them too early, praising preferred behaviour, or turning the session into a product demonstration.

Moderator question: What can I ask that helps me understand the participant's thinking without changing what they would naturally do?

Moderation essentials

Warm-upExplain the session and create a comfortable environment.
Neutral taskGive the scenario and goal without prescribing actions.
ObserveWatch actions, hesitation, errors and questions.
ProbeAsk open, non-leading follow-ups when useful.
Don't rescueAllow reasonable time for participants to attempt the task.
CloseAsk for reflections and explain next steps.

Apply

Moderate your Figma/Framer prototype test

Task

Write a short moderator guide with opening, scenario, task, neutral prompts, observation notes and closing questions.

Practice

Run a pilot with someone appropriate. Mark moments where you accidentally led, rescued or revealed an answer, then revise the script.

Separate what the participant did or said from your interpretation. This makes later synthesis more reliable.
