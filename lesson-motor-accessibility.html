57. Motor Accessibility

Design interactions that remain practical for people using different pointing, keyboard, switch or touch methods.

LEARN

Interaction should not depend on precision

Small targets, tightly packed controls, timed gestures and drag-only interactions can create barriers. Provide adequate target area, spacing, alternatives and forgiving interaction where appropriate.

Designer question: Can the task be completed without requiring precise, fast or sustained movement?

SEE
  • Provide sufficient target size and separation.
  • Avoid requiring simultaneous or rapid gestures when alternatives are possible.
  • Offer keyboard or non-drag alternatives to essential drag actions.
  • Prevent accidental destructive activation where possible.
  • Respect the difference between visual size and actual interactive area.

APPLY — FIGMA / FRAMER

Audit one flow for tiny targets, drag-only actions, timed interactions and accidental activation. Prototype larger hit areas and at least one alternative input path.
