65. RTL & Bidirectional UX

Learn how right-to-left languages change reading order, alignment, navigation and directional UI.

Learn

Separate semantic direction from decorative alignment. Mirror directional relationships where appropriate, while keeping familiar symbols or data conventions when they should not mirror.

Check

Does the layout preserve meaning and interaction when direction changes?

Figma / Framer Practice

Create LTR and RTL variants of navigation, forms and directional controls. Record what mirrors and what stays consistent.

Knowledge Check

What is the right approach?

  1. Mirror every pixel automatically.
  2. Adapt directional relationships according to meaning and context.
  3. Never support RTL.

Answer: 2.
