Accessibility by Design

Learn to design interfaces that remain understandable and usable across different abilities, devices, contexts and input methods.

Accessibility is part of good UX

Accessibility means considering how people with different visual, motor, hearing and cognitive needs can perceive, understand, navigate and operate an interface. It should be considered during design, not added only at the end.

Designer question: If color, precise pointing, small text or visual context is unavailable, can the user still understand and complete the task?
