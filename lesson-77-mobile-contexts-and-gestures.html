77. Mobile Contexts & Gestures

Design touch interactions that work across device sizes, contexts, reach, orientation and input variability.

Learn

Do not make essential actions depend on obscure gestures. Provide visible alternatives, adequate targets, clear feedback and layouts that respect reachable areas and changing viewport conditions.

Check

Can a person discover and complete the task without knowing a hidden gesture?

Figma / Framer Practice

Prototype a mobile flow with tap, swipe and long-press candidates. Replace any essential hidden gesture with a discoverable alternative.

Knowledge Check

What is safer for an essential action?

  1. Only an undiscoverable gesture.
  2. A visible control or discoverable interaction with appropriate feedback.
  3. A gesture with no feedback.

Answer: 2.
