Color & Contrast

Learn how color communicates meaning, creates emphasis and supports readability—and why color should not be the only signal users rely on.

Color has a job

In interfaces, color can establish hierarchy, communicate state, distinguish actions and reinforce brand expression. A strong system also works when color is unavailable, reduced, or interpreted differently.

Designer question: If the color disappeared, would the user still understand what the element means or what action is available?
