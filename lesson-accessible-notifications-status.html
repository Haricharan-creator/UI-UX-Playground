Accessible Notifications & Status

Learn how to communicate system status, updates, progress and alerts without overwhelming people or making important information dependent on one sensory channel.

Learn

Feedback should arrive at the right time

A status message can be useful, distracting or inaccessible depending on timing, priority and presentation. Designers need to distinguish information that can wait from information that requires immediate attention.

Designer question: If a person misses the visual change, sound or animation, will they still understand the important status and know what to do next?

Status and notification essentials

PriorityMatch interruption level to the importance of the information.
PersistenceKeep important information available long enough to understand or revisit it.
LocationPlace feedback near the relevant context where possible.
ProgressCommunicate meaningful progress and completion states clearly.
Non-visual meaningDo not rely only on colour, animation or sound for critical status.
RecoveryExplain what happened and provide a useful next action when something fails.

Apply

Audit feedback in Figma or Framer

Task

Choose a flow with loading, success, warning or error feedback. Classify each message by urgency and identify what happens if it is missed.

Deliver

Design contextual feedback, progress and recovery states. Document timing, persistence, interruption level and the user's next action.

Validate implemented behaviour with realistic timing and repeated use. A visually attractive toast or banner is not automatically accessible.
