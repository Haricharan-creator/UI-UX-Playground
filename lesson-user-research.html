User Research

Learn how to understand people, contexts, needs and behaviours before making important design decisions.

Learn

Research reduces assumptions

User research helps designers learn about people's goals, behaviours, pain points and context. The method should match the question: interviews can explore motivations, observation can reveal behaviour, surveys can gather broader responses, and usability testing can evaluate an experience.

Designer question: What do I actually know, what am I assuming, and what evidence would reduce the uncertainty before I make this design decision?

Evidence vs assumption

Assumption

“Users probably want a dashboard with six shortcuts.”

This is a hypothesis, not evidence.

Research finding

“In five observed sessions, participants repeatedly looked for account status before starting the next task.”

This is an observation that can inform a design decision, with appropriate research limitations.

Task

Output
