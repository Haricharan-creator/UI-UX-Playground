Personas should connect to evidence

A persona is a communication tool that summarises meaningful patterns in user needs, behaviours, goals and constraints. It should be grounded in research and used to support design decisions—not treated as a fictional biography.

Designer question: Which parts of this persona are supported by evidence, and how will this representation help us make a specific design decision?
