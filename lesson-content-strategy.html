Content Strategy for UX

Learn how content helps people understand, decide and act. Good UX content is purposeful, clear, accessible and connected to the user's task.

Learn

Content is part of the interface

Labels, headings, instructions, error messages, empty states and calls to action all shape the experience. Content strategy asks what information people need, when they need it, and how it should be structured and maintained.

Designer question: What does the user need to understand at this moment to confidently complete the task?
See

Clearer content reduces uncertainty

Vague

“Submit”

“Something went wrong.”

“Enter details.”

Low context; users may not know the outcome, problem or expected information.
Contextual

“Create account”

“We couldn't save your changes. Check your connection and try again.”

“Enter your work email address.”

Labels and messages communicate action, context and next steps.
