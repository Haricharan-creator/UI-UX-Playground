UX Microcopy

Learn how small pieces of interface language—buttons, labels, hints, confirmations and errors—can reduce uncertainty and help people act with confidence.

Learn

Small words, important decisions

Microcopy is the short text that guides people through an interface. Good microcopy is clear, concise, contextual and respectful. It tells users what an action does, what information is needed, or what happens next.

Designer question: If the user reads only this small piece of text, will they understand the action, consequence or next step?

Vague

“Continue”

“Invalid input.”

“Delete?”

Limited context can make the result or recovery path unclear.
Contextual

“Review order”

“Enter a valid work email, such as name@company.com.”

“Delete this draft? You won't be able to restore it.”

The language makes action, expectation and consequence clearer.

Microcopy challenge in Figma or Framer

Task

Choose one flow and identify five pieces of microcopy: CTA, field hint, error, confirmation and empty state.

Rewrite

Write clearer alternatives. Check action, context, consequence, tone and accessibility. Test the copy at realistic lengths.

Keep wording consistent with your content strategy and design system.
