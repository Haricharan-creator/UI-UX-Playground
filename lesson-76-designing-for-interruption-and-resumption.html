76. Designing for Interruption & Resumption

Design flows that remain recoverable when people switch apps, lose connection, receive interruptions or return later.

Learn

Preserve progress when appropriate, make saved state visible, distinguish drafts from completed work and restore users to a meaningful point in the task.

Check

Can a returning user understand what was saved and continue confidently?

Figma / Framer Practice

Prototype a long task across pause, backgrounding, timeout and return. Include draft, saved and expired states.

Knowledge Check

What supports resumption?

  1. Discard progress silently.
  2. Preserve appropriate progress and explain its current state.
  3. Restart every task.

Answer: 2.
