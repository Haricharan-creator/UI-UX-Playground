Empathy & Journey Mapping

Learn to understand an experience from the user's perspective and map what happens across a task so opportunities are grounded in evidence and context.

Learn

Map the experience, not just the screen

Journey mapping looks across the stages of an experience: what the user is trying to do, what they encounter, how they respond, where friction appears and what happens next. It helps designers see opportunities that a single screen may hide.

Designer question: What is the user's goal at this stage, what evidence tells us what they experience, and where does friction interrupt progress?
