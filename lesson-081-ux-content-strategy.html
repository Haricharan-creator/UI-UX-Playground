81. UX Content Strategy

Design content as part of the experience: identify user needs, content purpose, hierarchy, voice, states and ownership before polishing UI.

Learn

Useful content helps people understand, decide and act. Map what information is needed at each task step, then remove duplication and ambiguity.

Checklist

  • Purpose and audience
  • Information hierarchy
  • Plain, task-focused language
  • Content states and edge cases
  • Ownership and maintenance

Knowledge check

What should happen first?

Figma / Framer practice

Pick one flow. Create a content map, identify missing/error/empty states, then prototype the improved hierarchy. Compare V1 and V2 with a short usability check.

Loop: V1 → Review → V2 → Validate → Reflect
