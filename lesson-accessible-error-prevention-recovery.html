Accessible Error Prevention & Recovery

Learn how to prevent avoidable mistakes, make risky actions understandable and help people recover without unnecessary loss of work.

Learn

Good error UX starts before the error

Not every mistake should be handled only after submission. Clear constraints, sensible defaults, confirmation for consequential actions and timely feedback can reduce avoidable errors while preserving user control.

Designer question: What can we do before, during and after an action to prevent a costly mistake and make recovery straightforward?
See

Error prevention essentials

Clear constraintsExplain formats, limits and requirements before input or commitment.
Helpful defaultsUse sensible defaults without silently making consequential choices for the user.
UndoWhere practical, provide a reversible path for accidental changes or deletion.
ConfirmationFor consequential actions, give enough context to confirm the intended choice.
Specific feedbackExplain what went wrong and what the person can do next.
Preserve workAvoid clearing useful data or forcing unnecessary repetition after an error.
