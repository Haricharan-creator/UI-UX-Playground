UI/UX Foundations · Lesson 06

Visual Hierarchy

Learn how layout, size, spacing, contrast and emphasis guide a user's attention and make interfaces easier to understand.

1 · Learn

What is visual hierarchy?

Visual hierarchy is the way an interface communicates what deserves attention first, second and later. It helps users understand where to look, what matters and what action to take.

Key idea: Good hierarchy reduces the amount of effort a user needs to understand a screen. It is not simply making important things bigger; it is creating a deliberate relationship between emphasis, grouping and order.
