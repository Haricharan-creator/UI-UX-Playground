Learning rule
Feedback explains the evidence, the principle involved, and a practical rework direction. The learner remains responsible for the design decision.
Practice Review

Submission received

  • What is working: Your submission is ready for UX/UI review.
  • Next: inspect hierarchy, spacing, accessibility and interaction states against the task requirements.
  • Rework: return to Figma/Framer and make the improvements you believe are appropriate.
Next iteration
Submit V2 after your rework. HACHARA will use the before/after evidence to focus the next review on what changed and why.
