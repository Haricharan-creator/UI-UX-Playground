Information Architecture

Learn how to organise content, navigation and labels so people can understand where they are, find what they need and predict where an action will take them.

Learn

Structure information around user needs

Information architecture (IA) is about organising and labelling information. Good IA reduces the effort required to understand a product's structure and find the right content or action.

Designer question: Can a person predict where something belongs, what a label means, and where they will go next?
