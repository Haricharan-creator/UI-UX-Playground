82. Design Systems

Learn how reusable tokens, components, patterns and governance create consistency without removing thoughtful product-specific decisions.

Learn

  • Design tokens encode repeatable decisions.
  • Components combine structure, behaviour and states.
  • Patterns solve recurring user problems.
  • Documentation explains when and why to use them.
  • Governance keeps the system healthy as products evolve.
