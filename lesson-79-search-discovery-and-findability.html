79. Search, Discovery & Findability

Help people find relevant content, understand results and recover from searches that do not match their intent.

Learn

Use familiar terminology, useful suggestions, meaningful result summaries, filters that reflect the task and clear no-results guidance. Do not treat search as a substitute for information architecture.

Check

Can users formulate a query, understand the results and adjust the search when nothing useful appears?

Figma / Framer Practice

Prototype search idle, typing, results, filtered results, no results, error and cleared states for a realistic content set.

Knowledge Check

What makes no-results handling useful?

  1. Show a blank page.
  2. Explain the situation and suggest useful next steps.
  3. Remove the query silently.

Answer: 2.
