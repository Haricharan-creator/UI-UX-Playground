74. Designing for Recovery

Help people recover from mistakes, interruptions, failed requests and unexpected system conditions.

Learn

Preserve entered information where possible, explain the problem in task language, offer a specific next step and provide undo or retry when safe.

Check

After an error, can the user recover without restarting the entire task?

Figma / Framer Practice

Pick a multi-step form or upload flow. Prototype validation, network failure, retry, cancel and restored-progress states.

Knowledge Check

What makes recovery stronger?

  1. Discard all work.
  2. Explain the problem and provide a clear recovery path.
  3. Show only an error code.

Answer: 2.
