Good Design / Poor Design Learning

GOOD DESIGN

What works and why?

  • Clear information hierarchy
  • Predictable interaction
  • Consistent spacing and alignment
  • Readable typography
  • Appropriate contrast and states
  • Design decisions supported by context/evidence
COMMON PROBLEMS

What should we look for?

  • Competing visual hierarchy
  • Unclear affordances or labels
  • Inconsistent patterns
  • Excessive cognitive load
  • Weak error/feedback states
  • Accessibility barriers

Good Design / Poor Design Lab

Learn to explain design quality with evidence. HACHARA does not label a screen “good” or “bad” without context; it helps you identify strengths, risks and reasons.

GOOD DESIGN

What to notice

  • Clear hierarchy and task focus
  • Consistent patterns and spacing
  • Readable, accessible content
  • Useful feedback and recoverability
  • Responsive behaviour appropriate to context
COMMON PROBLEMS

What to investigate

  • Competing actions or unclear priority
  • Inconsistent interaction patterns
  • Weak contrast or legibility
  • Unexpected states or missing feedback
  • Layout problems at target sizes

Screen Critique Practice

Learning rule

Don't stop at “I like it” or “I don't like it.”

Describe the observation → connect it to a principle → explain the possible effect → identify what should be validated → propose a change → test the change.
