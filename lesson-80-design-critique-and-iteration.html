80. Design Critique & Iteration

Turn design review into evidence-based learning rather than personal preference or visual opinion.

Learn

Critique against user goals, requirements, UX principles, accessibility, evidence and product constraints. Separate observation from interpretation and recommendation.

Check

Can the reviewer explain what is happening, why it matters and what should be tested next?

Figma / Framer Practice

Create a design, share a screenshot, receive structured critique against UX/UI principles, revise it and submit V2 with a short rationale for each change.

Knowledge Check

What makes critique useful?

  1. “I like it.”
  2. Evidence-based observations connected to goals and actionable recommendations.
  3. Changing everything at once.

Answer: 2.

Rework

V1 → Screenshot → Critique → V2 → Validate → Reflect.
