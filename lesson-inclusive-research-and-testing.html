59. Inclusive Research & Testing

Turn accessibility and inclusion from assumptions into questions, observations and evidence.

LEARN

Do not design inclusion from imagination alone

Use research and testing to uncover barriers across abilities, devices, environments and experience levels. Avoid treating one participant as representative of everyone; document the specific context and evidence.

Designer question: What assumption can we test rather than debate?

SEE
  • Recruit participants relevant to the product and task.
  • Ask about real workflows and barriers.
  • Observe completion rather than relying only on opinions.
  • Test alternative interaction paths.
  • Record evidence and uncertainty separately from interpretation.
CHECK

Which is stronger research practice?

APPLY — FIGMA / FRAMER

Prototype one key task. Create a short test script, define success criteria and test the same flow under at least two different interaction conditions. Capture findings and make a V2 based on observed evidence.

Loop: V1 → Test → Evidence → V2 → Retest → Reflect
