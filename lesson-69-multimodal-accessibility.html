69. Multimodal Accessibility

Combine visual, text, audio and interaction channels so people can choose practical ways to perceive and operate a task.

Learn

Redundancy is useful when channels complement each other. Important status should not depend on one sensory or input path.

Check

If one channel is unavailable, can the user still understand and complete the essential task?

Figma / Framer Practice

Choose a critical flow and map visual, textual, audio and input alternatives. Remove single-channel dependencies.

Knowledge Check

What is multimodal accessibility?

  1. Adding more animation.
  2. Providing meaningful alternative ways to perceive and operate important tasks.
  3. Making every screen identical.

Answer: 2.
