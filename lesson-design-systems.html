A system is more than a component library

A design system connects reusable building blocks with design decisions, usage guidance and shared rules. The goal is not to make every screen identical; it is to make intentional patterns easier to reuse, maintain and evolve.

Designer question: Which decision should be made once and reused safely instead of being recreated differently on every screen?
