User Flows

Learn how to map the steps, decisions, system states and recovery paths a person experiences while completing a task.

Learn

Start with the user's goal

A user flow is more than a sequence of screens. It describes how a person moves from a goal through actions, decisions, feedback and outcomes. Include important alternate paths, errors and recovery when they affect the experience.

Designer question: What is the user's goal, what must they decide, and what happens when something goes wrong?
