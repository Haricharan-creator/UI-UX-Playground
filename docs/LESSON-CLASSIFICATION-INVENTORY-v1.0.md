## Purpose

This inventory assigns every physical lesson file a provisional curriculum role without modifying the lesson source files.

### Status meanings

- **core** — currently treated as a distinct learning unit.
- **supporting** — useful as a deeper/supporting treatment of another capability.
- **overlap-candidate** — related content requiring explicit curriculum comparison; **not a duplicate declaration**.
- **reference-template** — structural/reference material rather than a standalone curriculum topic.
- **unmapped** — physical content exists but its curriculum role cannot yet be established confidently.

### Role meanings

The role is descriptive and may be:
- foundation
- specialized-capability
- applied-practice
- supporting-depth
- measurement
- delivery
