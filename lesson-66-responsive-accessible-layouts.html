66. Responsive Accessible Layouts

Build layouts that remain usable across viewport sizes, zoom, orientation and content changes.

Learn

Use flexible containers, resilient spacing and reflow. Avoid horizontal scrolling for ordinary content and prevent clipping or overlapping when text grows.

Check

Can the task still be completed when the viewport becomes narrow or content becomes larger?

Figma / Framer Practice

Prototype desktop, tablet and mobile states. Stress-test long labels, larger text and landscape/portrait changes.

Knowledge Check

What characterizes a resilient responsive layout?

  1. Fixed coordinates everywhere.
  2. Content reflows without losing task access or meaning.
  3. Hide important controls on small screens.

Answer: 2.
