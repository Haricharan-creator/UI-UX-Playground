62. Designing for Children

Learn how comprehension, motor skills, privacy, safety and developmental differences affect children's interfaces.

Learn

Use age-appropriate language and interaction, clear feedback and safe defaults. Design with appropriate adult oversight and avoid manipulative patterns.

Check

Is the task understandable, safe, forgiving and appropriate for the intended developmental context?

Figma / Framer Practice

Redesign a child-facing flow. Map comprehension, mistakes, consent/guardian context and safe exit paths.

Knowledge Check

What should guide a child-facing design?

  1. Engagement at any cost.
  2. Developmental fit, safety, clarity and appropriate privacy.
  3. Adult interfaces with smaller text.

Answer: 2.
