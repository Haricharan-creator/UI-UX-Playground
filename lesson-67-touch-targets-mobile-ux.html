67. Touch Targets & Mobile UX

Design touch interactions that are easy to hit, distinguish and recover from.

Learn

Give controls sufficient target size and separation, keep important actions reachable, and avoid relying on tiny gestures or tightly packed controls.

Check

Can users accurately tap the intended control under realistic one-handed, moving or constrained conditions?

Figma / Framer Practice

Audit a mobile screen for target size, spacing, reachability and gesture alternatives. Prototype error recovery.

Knowledge Check

What reduces accidental activation?

  1. Smaller controls placed close together.
  2. Adequate targets, separation and clear feedback.
  3. Hidden labels.

Answer: 2.
