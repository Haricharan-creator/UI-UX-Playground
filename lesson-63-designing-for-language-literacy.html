63. Designing for Language & Literacy

Make interfaces understandable across reading levels, languages and communication preferences.

Learn

Prefer familiar words, direct instructions, meaningful headings and supportive examples. Avoid jargon when it is not necessary.

Check

Can a person understand the action, consequence and next step without expert vocabulary?

Figma / Framer Practice

Take a complex flow and rewrite labels, errors and instructions. Test expansion for translation.

Knowledge Check

What improves comprehension?

  1. Dense specialist language.
  2. Clear wording, useful hierarchy and contextual guidance.
  3. Icons without labels.

Answer: 2.
