72. Progressive Disclosure

Reveal complexity at the moment it becomes useful while keeping important information discoverable.

Learn

Separate essential decisions from advanced options. Use summaries, expandable sections and contextual details without hiding information required to complete the task.

Check

Does the interface show enough to act without forcing users through unnecessary detail?

Figma / Framer Practice

Redesign a settings or onboarding screen using essential-first content and optional advanced controls.

Knowledge Check

What is the goal?

  1. Hide all advanced information permanently.
  2. Expose useful detail at the point of need.
  3. Add more screens regardless of task.
