Accessible Forms & Validation

Learn how labels, instructions, grouping, error messages and recovery paths can make forms easier to understand and complete.

Learn

Prevent errors, then help people recover

Good form UX does more than validate data. It helps people understand what is required, enter information in the right format, recognise what went wrong and correct it without losing useful work.

Designer question: Can someone understand each field before entering data, identify an error after submission and fix it without guessing or starting again?

Accessible form essentials

Clear labelsKeep field purpose understandable and associated with the relevant input.
InstructionsExplain requirements and formats before or as they become relevant.
GroupingGroup related fields and choices so their relationship is clear.
Error locationMake it clear which field needs attention and what must change.
Error messageDescribe the problem and, where useful, how to correct it.
RecoveryPreserve entered information where appropriate and avoid unnecessary resets.
