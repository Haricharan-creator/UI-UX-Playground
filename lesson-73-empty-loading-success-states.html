73. Empty, Loading & Success States

Design system states that explain what the product is doing, what exists and what the user can do next.

Learn

Distinguish first-use empty states, no-results states, loading, partial loading, success and failure. Each state should provide appropriate context and a useful next action.

Check

Can users tell whether there is no data, data is loading or something failed?

Figma / Framer Practice

Choose a dashboard or list and prototype all meaningful states with realistic content and recovery actions.

Knowledge Check

Why separate empty and loading states?

  1. They communicate different system conditions.
  2. They are always identical.
  3. Neither needs explanation.

Answer: 1.
