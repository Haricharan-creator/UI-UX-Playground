Usability Findings & Synthesis

Learn how to turn session observations into clear, traceable findings without confusing what happened with what you think it means.

Learn

Evidence first, interpretation second

A useful finding connects an observed behaviour to its impact and the design opportunity. Keep participant evidence, researcher interpretation and recommendation distinct so the reasoning can be checked.

Finding formula: Evidence → pattern or issue → user impact → implication/opportunity. Add severity only when the criteria are defined and supported.
See

Finding structure

EvidenceWhat did participants actually do or say?
PatternWhat repeated behaviour or issue appears?
ImpactHow could this affect task success or experience?
ScopeWhich users, flow or context does the evidence cover?
OpportunityWhat should the team explore or improve?
ConfidenceWhat are the study limits and what still needs validation?

Apply

Synthesise your Figma/Framer test

Task

Review your session notes and group observations into patterns. Keep direct evidence separate from interpretation.

Deliver

Create 3–5 findings with evidence, impact, scope, opportunity and confidence/limitations. Link each finding to the relevant screen or flow.

Only prioritise after the findings are clear. Avoid turning a small sample into a universal claim.
