Accessible Color & Contrast

Learn how colour, contrast and visual emphasis affect readability and interaction, and why important meaning should not depend on colour alone.

Learn

Colour is a signal, not the only instruction

Colour can establish hierarchy and communicate states, but people perceive colour differently and screens vary by environment. Strong UX combines sufficient contrast with text, icons, labels, patterns or other cues when meaning matters.

Designer question: If colour disappeared from the interface, could a person still understand what is interactive, selected, required, successful or wrong?

Colour accessibility essentials

Text contrastChoose text and background combinations that support comfortable reading.
UI contrastMake important controls, boundaries and states distinguishable.
Not colour alonePair colour with labels, icons, patterns or position when meaning depends on a state.
Focus statesMake keyboard focus visually distinct from the surrounding interface.
Error statesUse text or another clear cue in addition to a colour change.
Real contextsCheck contrast across themes, displays, lighting and content variations.
