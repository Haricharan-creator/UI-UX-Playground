71. Designing for Stress & Uncertainty

Design interfaces that remain understandable when users are anxious, rushed, uncertain or dealing with high-consequence tasks.

Learn

Reduce ambiguity, expose important status, use calm language, make next steps obvious and provide recovery paths. Do not rely on memory or hidden system state.

Check

Can a stressed user understand what happened, what happens next and how to recover?

Figma / Framer Practice

Choose a payment, support, travel or account-recovery flow. Create clear loading, success, failure and recovery states.

Knowledge Check

Which approach reduces uncertainty?

  1. Hide system status.
  2. Show meaningful status, next action and recovery options.
  3. Use vague warnings.

Answer: 2.
