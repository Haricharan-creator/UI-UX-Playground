Accessible Forms & Inputs

Learn how labels, instructions, grouping, validation and recovery make forms understandable and usable for people with different abilities and input methods.

Learn

Forms should explain the task, not test the user

A form becomes difficult when people have to guess what a field means, what format is expected or why an error occurred. Good form UX makes relationships and requirements clear before and after input.

Designer question: Can a person identify each field, understand what is required, enter the information, recover from mistakes and know when the form is complete?

Form accessibility essentials

LabelsGive every field a clear, persistent and meaningful label.
InstructionsExplain format, limits and requirements before they become a problem.
GroupingGroup related controls so their relationship and purpose are understandable.
Required statesMake required and optional fields clear without relying only on colour or symbols.
ValidationIdentify the affected field and explain how to correct the problem.
RecoveryPreserve useful entered data and avoid making users restart unnecessarily.

Task

Deliver
